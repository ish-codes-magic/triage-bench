# Triage comment style

A short draft that a maintainer could post (they will review it; nothing is posted
automatically).

- 1-3 sentences, plain and friendly, no greetings or sign-off.
- Say what you concluded and why in concrete terms: the module or file, the likely
  duplicate (`Possibly a duplicate of #123: same BadZipFile on empty archives.`), or
  exactly what is missing (`Could you share a minimal reproducer and the output of
  python -VV?`).
- Never promise a fix, a release, or a backport, and never assign blame.
- Do not repeat the issue back to the reporter.
