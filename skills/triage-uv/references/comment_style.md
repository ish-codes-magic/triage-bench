# Triage comment style

A short draft that a maintainer could post (they will review it; nothing is posted
automatically).

- 1-3 sentences, plain and friendly, no greetings or sign-off.
- Say what you concluded and why in concrete terms: the command or crate involved, the
  likely duplicate (`Possibly a duplicate of #123: same lockfile error with conflicting
  extras.`), or exactly what is missing (`Could you share the command you ran, your
  pyproject.toml and the output with -v?`).
- Never promise a fix or a release, and never assign blame.
- Do not repeat the issue back to the reporter.
